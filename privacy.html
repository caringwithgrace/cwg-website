Your Privacy

Privacy policy

Confidentiality is one of our core values. Here is what this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Caring with Grace website, www.caringwithgrace.com. Information you share with us as a client is handled separately, under your service agreement and the privacy laws that apply to it.

Information you give us

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive what you enter: your first and last name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to give it, how you heard about us, and your message.

Please do not include health details or other sensitive information in the form. We will go over specifics together by phone.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects general information such as the pages you visit, the kind of device and browser you use, your approximate location (city or region), and the site that referred you. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. It does not tell us your name or give us your contact details.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your message and talk with you about how we may be able to help.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • To protect the contact form from spam and misuse.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers, and sending us a message does not add you to a mailing list.

Companies that help us run this site

A small number of service providers handle information on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare processes contact form submissions and runs the security check that keeps automated spam out.
  • Postmark delivers your form message to our office email.
  • Google provides our site analytics, the fonts used on our pages, and the YouTube video on our Aging Life Care page.
  • GitHub hosts the website and, like any web host, may log technical details such as your IP address.

Each of these companies handles information under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work. Google also offers a free browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

If you would like to see, correct or delete a message you sent us, call us at (214) 775-9969 and we will take care of it.

How long we keep messages

We keep contact form messages only as long as we need them to respond to you and to maintain our business records.

Links to other websites

Our site links to other websites, including our newsletter at caringconnectionnews.com and the resources we recommend. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date below.

Questions

Call us at (214) 775-9969, write to us at 6310 LBJ Freeway, Suite 219, Dallas, TX 75240, or use our contact page.
